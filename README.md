Sum up 25 November's world news in one image. A newspaper front page for
Wednesday, 25 November 2026: Trump proposes tariffs of 20–40% on 14 trading
partners and Wall Street slides — Nvidia closes down 3.91% at $149.30 as
October inflation runs at 3.8%; tankers hit by mines catch fire in the
Strait of Hormuz; Shenzhou-24 docks at Tiangong carrying Pakistan's
Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign astronaut aboard the station; plus
the day's markets and briefs from Huawei, Ras Tanura, Cairo, Jerusalem,
Honiara, Dhaka and Sudan.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) is built by `compose.py` from three
editorial panels in `panels/` and the front-page lettering drawn with PIL.
Every number on the page was verified against reporting of 25 November 2026.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) validates
`entry.png` and writes it to `/output/entry.png`. It runs twice with
identical bytes.
